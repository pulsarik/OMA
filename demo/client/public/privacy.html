Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

1. Contact

Omaha Hi-Lo Poker is operated by the site owner. For privacy questions, requests to access, correct or erase data, and objections, contact pulsarik@gmail.com.

2. Information processed

The game works without account registration. Depending on how you use it, the service processes your chosen player nickname, browser identifiers (including cookies and local browser storage), table and game activity, hand histories, game statistics, error reports you submit, and connection metadata such as IP address, browser user agent, platform, screen and viewport dimensions, approximate country and referral/source information. Invitations and game links can contain access tokens; do not share private invitation links publicly.

Essential cookies and browser storage help remember your nickname, browser, table and seat so you can reconnect. These are not an account or a payment mechanism.

3. Purposes and legal grounds

We use game and technical data to operate tables, maintain gameplay and reconnect sessions (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b), where applicable), and to secure, troubleshoot and improve the service and understand usage (legitimate interests, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f), subject to the required balancing). Where an optional feature requires consent under applicable law, it must not be enabled without that consent. No real-money gambling or payments are provided.

4. Hosting and recipients

The website and game backend are deployed using Render hosting. Technical information is processed by hosting/network infrastructure to deliver and protect the service. Optional voice-chat and AI commentary features may involve additional service providers; their applicable processing terms and data flows should be reviewed before enabling these features. We do not sell player data.

5. Retention

Waiting tables are normally removed after approximately one hour if the game does not start. Browser cookies used for the player identifier, nickname and selected number of seats can be set for up to one year on your device, but can be deleted earlier in browser settings. Completed games and analytics are stored in the backend; automatic one-year deletion for these records is planned but is not yet confirmed to be active. Until it is deployed, these records can remain longer. You may request deletion using the contact above. This section will be updated when the retention mechanism is deployed and verified.

6. Your rights

Under applicable data-protection law, you may have rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and to withdraw consent where processing relies on it. You may also complain to a competent data-protection supervisory authority. Because there are no registered accounts, please provide enough information to identify the relevant table or session without sending passwords or private tokens unnecessarily.

7. Changes

We will update this notice when processing activities or retention rules change. Please check the date above.
